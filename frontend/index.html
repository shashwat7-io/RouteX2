    STEP 1

    🚗 If Trapped in a Vehicle

    • Do NOT stay in path: If debris flow or mudflow approaches, exit vehicle immediately.
    • Move Perpendicular: Run sideways (perpendicular) to the slide direction, toward high stable ground.
    • If trapped inside car: Roll into a tight fetal position, cover your head, and brace against car floor frame.
    STEP 2

    🏃 If Trapped On Foot Outdoors

    • Never run downhill: Landslide debris moves faster than human sprinting speed (up to 50 km/h).
    • Seek Solid Cover: Move behind large rock formations or thick trees out of the mud channel.
    • Protection Pose: If falling rocks approach, curl into a ball and shield your skull with your arms/backpack.
    STEP 3

    📻 Post-Slide Survival Rules

    • Watch for Secondary Slides: Water saturation often triggers secondary slope failures minutes later.
    • Check for Gas/Power Lines: Stay clear of fallen power lines and ruptured gas lines in mud.
    • Signal Rescuers: Use a whistle, mirror, flash, or heavy rhythmic drumming on metal to signal search dogs.
